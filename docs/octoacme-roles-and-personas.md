# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and coordinate quality practices so that work meets acceptance criteria and is ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Design test strategies and execute manual testing
- Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate security scanning and performance testing
- Work with Developers on test execution and defect resolution
- Report quality metrics and risks to the Project Manager and Product Manager
- Participate in sprint planning and demos

### Goals
- Ensure quality standards are met before release
- Reduce production defects
- Maintain test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, acceptance criteria, and defect reports with Developers
- Quality metrics and release-readiness updates to the Project Manager and Product Manager
- Sprint planning, demos, and test-result summaries

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic oversight across multiple projects or products. Unlike Product Managers, who own product vision and backlog priorities, Product Leads resolve portfolio-level conflicts and make final trade-off decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Provide strategic guidance to Product Managers
- Resolve risks and conflicts between initiatives and unblock cross-team dependencies
- Approve resource allocation decisions and act as final authority on trade-offs
- Receive escalations from Project Managers and communicate with executive sponsors

### Goals
- Ensure portfolio alignment
- Unblock cross-team dependencies
- Maintain strategic roadmap consistency

### Typical Communication
- Escalations and delivery risks from Project Managers
- Strategic guidance and priority alignment with Product Managers
- Portfolio decisions and updates with executive sponsors

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive-level business context, resources, and decision-making authority to ensure projects support organizational objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the project charter and provide business context and constraints
- Make high-level prioritization decisions and remove organizational barriers
- Approve major scope changes and provide budget or resource oversight
- Participate in project initiation and closure

### Goals
- Ensure business objectives are met
- Maintain budget and resource control
- Minimize risk to business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone updates and escalations from the Project Manager
- Major scope and priority decisions with the Product Manager and Product Lead
- Project initiation, approval, and closure discussions

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads/Architects (used as needed based on project size) guide system design and technical direction while supporting Developers in delivering reliable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and conduct design reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor Developers and collaborate with them on technical decisions
- Advise the Product Manager on technical feasibility
- Flag technical risks in weekly syncs

### Goals
- Maintain code quality and scalability
- Reduce technical debt
- Ensure system reliability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical decisions with Developers
- Feasibility advice and risk updates to the Product Manager and Project Manager
- Technical risk discussions in weekly syncs

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers guide security and compliance practices throughout delivery and confirm that releases meet applicable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and conduct or coordinate security scanning
- Participate in planning to identify security requirements
- Review pull requests and releases and approve releases from a compliance perspective
- Respond to security incidents and handle security escalations

### Goals
- Ensure compliance and data protection
- Reduce security vulnerabilities
- Maintain an audit trail

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and guidance with the Product Manager, Project Manager, and Developers during planning
- Findings and remediation follow-up in pull request reviews
- Release approvals and incident or escalation updates with the Project Manager and relevant stakeholders
